Cologne Pack 🇩🇪

The on-the-ground cheat sheet. Save it to your phone before you fly.

Passport only ✅ € cash for stalls Mug deposits
💶

Money

Euros. Cards work in hotels, restaurants and most shops, but plenty of market stalls are still cash only.

  • Bring a cash float for Glühwein, food and mug deposits.
  • Every drink comes in a deposit mug: return it for the cash back, or keep it.
  • Tipping: round up or about 5–10% in restaurants, not at stalls.
📱

Data & apps

UK roaming or an eSIM covers it. Google Maps works well here.

  • DB Navigator for trains, including the Sunday airport run.
  • KVB app for Cologne trams and buses.
  • Check your own plan's EU roaming charges before you go.
🚶

Getting around

The Old Town markets are all walkable from the cathedral. Neumarkt, Rudolfplatz and Stadtgarten are a longer walk west, or a short tram ride.

  • Cologne Bonn airport → Köln Hbf: S-Bahn S19/S13, about 15–20 min.
  • Düsseldorf airport → Köln Hbf: SkyTrain to the airport station, then a train, about 40–55 min.

🆘 If something goes wrong

🚓

Emergency numbers

  • 112 — Fire & ambulance (and general EU emergency)
  • 110 — Police
  • Save the hotel address and the group chat offline.
🛂

Entry & admin

  • Bring your passport and check it's valid well beyond the trip.
  • Check whether the EU Entry/Exit System or ETIAS rules apply to your passport before you fly.
  • Keep a photo of your passport and boarding passes offline.

🧳 Good to know

🧥

Early-December weather

Cold and often damp. Plan for roughly 0–7°C, drizzle likely and dark by about 16:30. Thermal base layer, warm waterproof coat, hat, gloves and waterproof shoes.

🔌

Plugs & power

Type C / F sockets, 230V. UK plugs don't fit, so bring an adapter each.

🍺

Kölsch etiquette

  • Kölsch comes in small 0.2 l glasses and the waiter (Köbes) keeps bringing them.
  • Put your beer mat on top of the glass when you're done.
  • Tally marks on your beer mat are your bill.

🗣️ A few phrases

🙂

The basics

  • Hallo / Guten Tag · hello
  • Danke (schön) · thank you
  • Ja / Nein · yes / no
  • Entschuldigung · sorry / excuse me
🍷

At the market

  • Einen Glühwein, bitte · one mulled wine, please
  • Mit Schuss · with a shot of rum or amaretto
  • Was kostet das? · how much is it?
  • Prost! · cheers! (Kölsch: Kölle Alaaf is the carnival cry)
